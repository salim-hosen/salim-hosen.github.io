CASE STUDY

Asoug.com: Multi-Vendor E-commerce Platform

Building an end-to-end B2B and multi-vendor marketplace completely from scratch.

Laravel Vue.js MySQL

The Problem

Creating a scalable digital marketplace requires balancing complex logistics, vendor management, and user experience. Asoug.com needed to serve as both a B2B platform and a multi-vendor retail hub, allowing distinct businesses to manage their own storefronts seamlessly.

Architecture & Development

  • Sole Developer: Engineered the entire platform independently from the ground up, handling both frontend architecture and complex backend logic.
  • Multi-Vendor Ecosystem: Built sophisticated vendor dashboards allowing individual businesses to manage inventory, pricing, and fulfillment within a unified marketplace.
  • B2B Capabilities: Implemented specialized wholesale functionality, including tiered pricing, bulk ordering flows, and customized invoicing systems.
  • Tech Stack: Utilized Laravel for a secure, robust API and business logic layer, paired with a dynamic Vue.js frontend for a seamless shopping experience.
